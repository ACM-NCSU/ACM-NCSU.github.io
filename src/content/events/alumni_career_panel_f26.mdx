## About
NC State ACM and App Dev Club are hosting recent NC State CSC graduates working at Deutsche Bank, Lucid Software, and Fidelity to share their experiences going from college student to industry professional. They'll cover:
- Their day-to-day roles
- Navigating the job search
- Preparing for interviews
- Breaking into the tech industry

There will also be an opportunity to ask questions!
