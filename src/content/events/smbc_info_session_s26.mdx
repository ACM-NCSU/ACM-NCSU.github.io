## About
Interested in exploring tech careers at Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation? Join ACM @ NC State for an afternoon with SMBC representatives to learn about career opportunities, ask questions, and network with industry professionals.

SMBC is one of the largest banking groups in the world, offering a wide range of financial services, which include investment banking, sales & trading, corporate banking, and capital markets.
