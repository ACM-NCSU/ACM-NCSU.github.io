## About
ACM is introducing our very own hackathon! WolfHacks is a chance to form new connections, grow your network, and build a full software project from scratch, all in one weekend.

- **When:** Oct. 3 - 4, 2026, with a 24 hour building period
- **Where:** Centennial Campus
- **Cost:** Free
- **Who:** All majors, all skill levels
- **Teams:** 2-4 people

Expect prizes, mentors, workshops, and free food.

## Registration
Registration opens on August 24th. For more info, check out the official website: [wolfhacks.org](https://wolfhacks.org/)

Got any questions? Ask us in Discord or send an email to [acmchapter-org@ncsu.edu](mailto:acmchapter-org@ncsu.edu).
